# AI Adaptation & Content Engine

The AI subsystem abstracts OpenAI and Gemini models to provide:
1. Intelligent Caption Rewriting (adapted per platform style & character limit)
2. Tone Adjustment (Professional, Funny, Luxury, Viral, etc.)
3. Automated Hashtag Generation
4. Video Scene & Hook Analysis
5. Pre-publish Content Score (0-100) with actionable recommendations.
